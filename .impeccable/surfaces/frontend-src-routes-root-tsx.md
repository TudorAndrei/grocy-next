## Direction contract

THESIS: The pantry as a shelf of labelled enamel canisters — every product shows how full it is against its minimum. Refuses the grey admin template with stat tiles and a teal accent.

OWN-WORLD: White enamel ground (#f3f4ef), cobalt rim (#1d3a8a) as the shell: rail, rules, primary buttons, gauge fill. Black lettering. Archivo: expanded 800 caps for canister labels (page titles, panel heads), normal width for UI. Pill buttons with 1.5px cobalt outline. State inks: tomato expired, mustard due, green ok, violet agent. Raises — Labanotation: due bars measure real days left on one 14-day scale. J-card: amounts right-aligned tabular, unit beside. Saville: nothing labelled twice, no repeating stat tiles. Datamatics: data at full contrast, grey only for labels. Transit: one fixed marker colour per domain (stock cobalt, shopping mustard, home green, agent violet). Goods catalog: fixed furniture per product row.

STORY: Owner opens the app, sees what needs them today, acts in one tap or ⌘K, and sees and undoes what the agent did.
